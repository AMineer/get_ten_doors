Restoring a purchase

If you previously purchased the Ten Doors portfolio unlock, use the Restore Purchase option in the app while signed in to the same Apple Account used for the original purchase.

Billing

App Store purchases are processed by Apple. Ten Doors does not receive or store your full payment card information.

Tax information

Ten Doors helps organize rental records and prepare exports. It does not provide tax, legal, or accounting advice. Review your records with a qualified tax professional when appropriate.
